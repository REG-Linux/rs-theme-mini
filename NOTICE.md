# NOTICE

## Where the art comes from

`v2/art/` is made from the system artwork of the Canvas theme
([REG-Linux/es-theme-canvas](https://github.com/REG-Linux/es-theme-canvas), a fork of
[Siddy212/canvas-es](https://github.com/Siddy212/canvas-es)): the logos are copied as they are,
the console pictures are reduced to pixel art by `tools/gen_assets.py`. Canvas's own NOTICE
records that its licence is unresolved (its LICENSE says CC0 1.0, its README CC BY-NC-SA 2.0) and
that the more restrictive applies until settled. This theme follows it: **CC BY-NC-SA 2.0**,
attribution to Siddy212 and the Canvas authors, non-commercial, shared alike.

No artwork of any other theme is used. The layout follows the general style of the mini consoles'
menus; no asset of those consoles or of other themes made after them is copied.
